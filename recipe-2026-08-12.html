Golden Hour Miso-Corn Pasta with Seared Scallops

Published: Wednesday, August 12, 2026 | By Elena Vance

Happy Wednesday, everyone! We are officially in the heart of August, that magical "golden hour" of summer where the produce is so vibrant it practically cooks itself. Today, August 12th, the humidity is high here in the city, but the local sweet corn is at its absolute peak—snappy, sugary, and full of sunshine.

This recipe is my love letter to late summer. It combines the deep, salty umami of white miso with the natural sweetness of charred corn. We’re finishing it with buttery seared scallops to make it feel like a true celebration of the season. It’s sophisticated enough for a dinner party but fast enough for a weeknight treat.

Recipe: Miso-Corn & Scallop Bucatini

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 20 mins Serves: 2-3

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Bucatini or Spaghetti
  • 2 ears of fresh sweet corn, kernels sliced off the cob
  • 6-8 large sea scallops (dry-packed)
  • 2 tbsp White Miso paste
  • 3 tbsp Unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 shallots, finely minced
  • 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup pasta water (reserved)
  • 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • Handful of fresh Thai basil or cilantro, torn
  • 1/2 tsp red chili flakes
  • Kosher salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the bucatini until just shy of al dente (usually 1 minute less than the package directions). Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Sear the Scallops: Pat the scallops extremely dry with paper towels and season with salt. In a large skillet over high heat, add 1 tablespoon of butter. Once foaming, add scallops. Sear for 2 minutes per side until a deep golden crust forms. Remove from pan and set aside.
  3. Char the Corn: In the same skillet, add the corn kernels. Let them sit undisturbed for 2-3 minutes to get a nice char. Stir and cook for another 2 minutes. Remove about 1/4 cup of the charred corn for garnish.
  4. Build the Sauce: Lower the heat to medium. Add the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter, shallots, and garlic. Sauté for 2 minutes until fragrant. Whisk the miso paste into 1/2 cup of the warm pasta water until smooth, then pour it into the skillet.
  5. Combine: Toss the cooked pasta into the miso-corn sauce. Increase heat slightly and toss vigorously, adding more pasta water if needed, until the sauce becomes glossy and coats every strand. Stir in the lime juice and chili flakes.
  6. Serve: Divide the pasta into bowls. Top with the seared scallops, reserved charred corn, and fresh herbs. Serve immediately while the steam carries that incredible miso aroma.
Pro Tips:
  • The Scallop Secret: Always use "dry" scallops. "Wet" scallops are treated with phosphates that prevent them from searing properly and give them a soapy taste.
  • Miso Choice: I prefer White (Shiro) Miso for this dish because it’s milder and sweeter. Red miso can be a bit too overpowering for the delicate corn.
  • No Scallops? This pasta is equally delicious with grilled shrimp or even a crispy soft-boiled egg on top.
