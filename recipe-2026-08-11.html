Charred Peach & Burrata Crostini

By The Savory Table Editorial

It’s the peak of August, and if your kitchen counter isn't currently overflowing with ripe, fragrant peaches, let this be your sign to head to the farmer's market. Today, we're celebrating the dog days of summer with a dish that hits every single flavor note: the smokiness of charred fruit, the creamy indulgence of fresh burrata, the salty bite of prosciutto, and a lingering kick of hot honey. It’s elegant enough for a late-summer garden party but simple enough for a Tuesday evening snack on the patio.

Prep Time: 10 mins
Cook Time: 10 mins
Servings: 4-6 (Appetizer)

Ingredients

  • 1 large French baguette, sliced into 1/2-inch rounds
  • 3 ripe but firm yellow peaches, pitted and sliced into wedges
  • 2 balls of fresh Burrata cheese (room temperature)
  • 4 oz thinly sliced Prosciutto di Parma
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon hot honey (or regular honey with a pinch of red pepper flakes)
  • Fresh basil leaves, torn for garnish
  • Balsamic glaze for drizzling
  • Flaky sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the Grill: Preheat a grill pan or outdoor grill to medium-high heat. Lightly brush both sides of your baguette slices and peach wedges with olive oil.
  2. Toast the Bread: Place the baguette slices on the grill for about 1-2 minutes per side until light grill marks appear and the edges are crisp. Remove and set aside.
  3. Char the Peaches: Place the peach wedges on the grill. Cook for about 2-3 minutes per side without moving them, allowing the natural sugars to caramelize and create deep grill marks. Remove and let them cool slightly.
  4. Assemble the Base: Gently break open the burrata balls. Spread a generous dollop of the creamy interior onto each toasted baguette slice.
  5. Layer the Flavors: Top the cheese with a small ribbon of prosciutto and one or two charred peach wedges.
  6. The Finishing Touches: Drizzle each crostini with hot honey and a light swirl of balsamic glaze. Sprinkle with fresh basil, flaky sea salt, and a crack of black pepper.
  7. Serve: Arrange on a wooden board and serve immediately while the bread is still crisp and the peaches are warm.

Pro Tips for Success

  • The Peach Test: Ensure your peaches are "ripe-firm." If they are too soft, they will turn to mush on the grill. If they are too hard, they won't develop that signature sweetness.
  • Vegetarian Option: Simply omit the prosciutto! The combination of peach, honey, and balsamic is punchy enough to stand on its own.
  • Temperature Matters: Always take your burrata out of the fridge 20 minutes before serving. The creamy center (stracciatella) has a much better texture and flavor at room temperature.
