About the kitchen

Why Dastarkhwan exists

Dastarkhwan means the spread laid out for a meal — the cloth, the dishes, everyone gathered around it. That's the feeling this site is built around: recipes written the way they'd be explained to you in a home kitchen, not a professional one.

Every recipe here is written with exact ingredient quantities, real prep and cook times, and steps in the order they actually need to happen — no scrolling past a life story to find the ingredient list.

What you'll find here

  • Classic Pakistani mains, rice dishes, and curries — biryani, karahi, nihari, and more
  • BBQ and kebabs, snacks, and street food you can make at home
  • Desserts, with clear timing so nothing overcooks or underset

How recipes are written

Each recipe lists ingredients first, then numbered steps, then a short tip based on where the dish most commonly goes wrong. New recipes are added regularly.
