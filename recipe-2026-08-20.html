Late Summer Umami: Charred Corn & Miso-Butter Pasta

It is peak August, and the local market stalls are practically overflowing with sweet corn. While I love a classic cob with salt, today I'm bringing something a bit more sophisticated to the table. This dish marries the golden sweetness of late-summer corn with the deep, salty funk of white miso. It’s creamy without using heavy cream, smoky from the charred kernels, and takes less than 30 minutes to pull together.

Prep: 10 mins Cook: 20 mins Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450g) Bucatini or Spaghetti
  • 3 ears of fresh Sweet Corn, kernels cut off the cob (about 3 cups)
  • 2 cups Cherry Tomatoes
  • 4 tbsp Unsalted Butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp White Miso paste
  • 3 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp Red Pepper Flakes
  • 1/2 cup Fresh Basil, torn
  • 1/2 cup Grated Pecorino Romano or Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the Pasta: Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta 1-2 minutes shy of al dente. Crucial: Reserve at least 1.5 cups of the starchy pasta water before draining.
  2. Char the Vegetables: While the pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet or cast iron pan over medium-high heat. Add the corn kernels and cherry tomatoes. Let them sit undisturbed for 3-4 minutes until the corn is charred and the tomatoes begin to blister and pop.
  3. Aromatics: Reduce heat to medium. Stir in the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for 1 minute until the garlic is fragrant but not browned.
  4. Create the Miso-Butter: In a small bowl, mash the miso paste into the softened butter until fully combined.
  5. Emulsify: Add the drained pasta directly into the skillet with the corn and tomatoes. Add the miso-butter and 1 cup of the reserved pasta water. Toss vigorously over medium heat. The butter and miso will melt, combining with the starchy water to create a glossy, silk-like sauce that coats the noodles.
  6. Finishing Touches: Remove from heat. Fold in the grated cheese and fresh basil. If the pasta looks too dry, add a splash more pasta water. Season with freshly cracked black pepper.

Chef's Tips

  • The Miso: Use white (Shiro) miso for this recipe. Red miso is too fermented and will overpower the delicate sweetness of the fresh corn.
  • Don't Scrimp on Water: That cloudy pasta water is liquid gold. It's the only thing that turns the miso and butter into a cohesive sauce rather than a greasy mess.
  • Vegan Option: Swap the butter for a vegan butter alternative or even tahini, and use nutritional yeast instead of cheese for a similar umami punch.
