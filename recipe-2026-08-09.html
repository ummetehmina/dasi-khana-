Golden Hour Miso Corn Pasta

There is a specific kind of magic in the air this Sunday. The August heat is starting to lean into that mellow, late-summer glow, and the farmer's market stalls are practically overflowing with sweet corn. Today’s recipe is a tribute to this "Golden Hour" of the season. We’re taking the natural sweetness of peak-summer corn and pairing it with the deep, salty umami of white miso butter. It’s a 20-minute meal that tastes like a luxury sunset on a plate.

Recipe: Miso-Butter Corn Pappardelle

Prep: 15 mins Cook: 15 mins Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340g) Pappardelle or Fettuccine pasta
  • 3 large ears of fresh sweet corn (kernels cut off the cob)
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp white (shiro) miso paste
  • 2 large shallots, finely minced
  • 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup pasta cooking water (reserved)
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Fresh basil leaves and cracked black pepper for garnish

Instructions

  1. Boil the Pasta: Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to package instructions until just shy of al dente. Important: Reserve at least 1 cup of starchy pasta water before draining.
  2. Sauté the Aromatics: While the pasta boils, melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the minced shallots and cook for 3-4 minutes until translucent. Stir in the garlic and red pepper flakes, cooking for another minute until fragrant.
  3. Char the Corn: Increase the heat to medium-high. Add the corn kernels to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 5-6 minutes until the corn is tender and some kernels are starting to turn golden brown.
  4. Create the Miso Sauce: Reduce the heat to low. Add the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter and the miso paste. Stir vigorously to break up the miso and incorporate it into the butter.
  5. Emulsify: Add the cooked pasta directly into the skillet along with 1/4 cup of the reserved pasta water. Toss continuously, adding the Parmesan cheese and more pasta water as needed, until a glossy, creamy sauce coats every strand of pasta.
  6. Finish and Serve: Season with plenty of fresh black pepper (the miso usually provides enough salt, so taste first!). Garnish with torn fresh basil and an extra sprinkle of Parmesan.

Pro Tips for Success

  • Don't Waste the Cob: After cutting off the kernels, run the back of your knife down the cob to "milk" it. Add that corn milk directly into your skillet for extra sweetness and creaminess.
  • Miso Quality: Use white or yellow miso for this. Red miso is fermented longer and can be too overpowering for the delicate sweetness of the corn.
  • Texture Contrast: For an extra crunch, top the dish with toasted panko breadcrumbs mixed with a little lemon zest.
