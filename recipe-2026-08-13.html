The Golden Hour: Miso-Butter Scallops with Charred Summer Corn

There is a specific quality to the light in mid-August—a heavy, honeyed glow that signals the peak of summer bounty. Today, the humidity broke just enough to let a breeze through the kitchen, and the farmer's market was overflowing with sweet corn so fresh you could eat it raw off the cob.

In celebration of this "golden hour" of the season, I’m sharing a recipe that feels incredibly sophisticated but comes together in under twenty minutes. We’re pairing succulent, pan-seared sea scallops with a savory, umami-rich miso butter and smoky charred corn. It’s sweet, salty, and tastes exactly like a coastal sunset.

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 8-10 Large Sea Scallops: Look for "dry-packed" scallops for the best sear.
  • 2 Ears of Fresh Corn: Kernels cut off the cob.
  • 1 cup Snap Peas: Trimmed and halved diagonally.
  • 2 tbsp White Miso Paste: For that perfect salty-sweet umami kick.
  • 3 tbsp Unsalted Butter: Divided.
  • 1 tbsp Neutral Oil: Grapeseed or avocado oil works best for high heat.
  • 1 clove Garlic: Grated or finely minced.
  • Fresh Cilantro & Lime Wedges: For garnish and brightness.
  • Pinch of Red Chili Flakes: Optional, for a tiny bit of heat.

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Scallops: Remove the small side muscle from the scallops if still attached. Pat them extremely dry with paper towels on all sides. Season lightly with salt (remember the miso is salty!).
  2. Make the Miso Butter: In a small bowl, mash 2 tablespoons of softened butter with the miso paste and minced garlic until smooth. Set aside.
  3. Char the Veggies: Heat a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat with the remaining 1 tablespoon of butter. Add the corn kernels and snap peas. Cook undisturbed for 2 minutes to get a nice char, then toss and cook for another 2 minutes. Stir in half of your miso butter mixture until melted and glazing the vegetables. Transfer to a plate and keep warm.
  4. Sear the Scallops: Wipe out the skillet and return to high heat with the neutral oil. Once the oil is shimmering (almost smoking), add the scallops. Do not crowd the pan. Sear undisturbed for 2 minutes until a deep golden-brown crust forms.
  5. The Finish: Flip the scallops. Add the remaining miso butter to the pan. Use a spoon to baste the scallops with the melting butter for about 1 minute. The scallops should be opaque but still tender.
  6. Assemble: Divide the charred corn and snap pea mixture between two plates. Top with the seared scallops and drizzle any remaining miso butter from the pan over the top. Garnish with cilantro and a generous squeeze of lime.
Pro-Tip: The secret to a restaurant-quality sear is moisture control. If your scallops are wet, they will steam instead of brown. Use more paper towels than you think you need! Also, ensure your skillet is screaming hot before the scallops hit the surface.
