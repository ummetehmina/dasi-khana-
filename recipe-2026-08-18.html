Charred Peach & Burrata Crostini

By Elena Vance | The Seasonal Table

There is a specific kind of magic that happens in mid-August. The sun hangs heavy and golden, and the peaches are so ripe they practically perfume the entire kitchen the moment you bring them home from the market. Today’s recipe is a tribute to this fleeting transition from late summer to early autumn. We’re taking peak-season yellow peaches, giving them a quick sear to caramelize those natural sugars, and pairing them with creamy burrata and a drizzle of spicy "hot" honey. It’s the ultimate Tuesday evening appetizer—or, if you’re like me, a perfectly acceptable light dinner with a glass of crisp Rosé.

Prep Time
10 mins
Cook Time
10 mins
Servings
4-6 People

Ingredients

  • 1 large French baguette, sliced into 1/2-inch rounds
  • 3 large ripe peaches, pitted and cut into thick wedges
  • 2 balls of fresh Burrata cheese (room temperature)
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes (adjust to heat preference)
  • A handful of fresh thyme leaves
  • Flaky sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper
  • Optional: Balsamic glaze for drizzling

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Hot Honey: In a small microwave-safe bowl (or a small saucepan), combine the honey and red pepper flakes. Heat for 20-30 seconds until thin and bubbly. Set aside to infuse while you prepare the rest of the dish.
  2. Toast the Crostini: Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Arrange baguette slices on a baking sheet, brush lightly with olive oil, and toast for 5-7 minutes until golden brown and crisp.
  3. Char the Peaches: Heat a grill pan or a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Lightly brush the peach wedges with a tiny bit of olive oil. Place them on the hot surface for 2 minutes per side, or until distinct grill marks appear and the fruit softens slightly. Remove from heat.
  4. Assemble: Carefully tear the burrata balls into bite-sized pieces. Spread a generous portion of the creamy cheese onto each toasted baguette slice.
  5. The Toppings: Top the cheese with one or two charred peach wedges. Drizzle the warm hot honey over the top.
  6. Final Flourish: Sprinkle with fresh thyme leaves, flaky sea salt, and a crack of black pepper. For an extra punch of acid, add a tiny drop of balsamic glaze.

Pro Tips for Success

  • Peach Ripeness: Choose peaches that are firm-ripe. If they are too soft, they will fall apart on the grill pan.
  • Room Temp Cheese: Always take your burrata out of the fridge 30 minutes before serving. The creamy center (stracciatella) needs to be soft and luscious, not cold and stiff.
  • Make it Vegan: Swap the burrata for a high-quality almond-based ricotta and use agave nectar instead of honey.
