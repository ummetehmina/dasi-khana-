The Golden Hour Peach & Burrata Salad

Monday, August 17, 2026

Happy Monday, everyone! As we lean into the final stretch of August, the air is thick with the scent of ripening fruit and the sun hangs just a little lower in the sky during our evening meals. Today is one of those quintessential late-summer afternoons where it’s simply too hot to turn on the oven, but the appetite demands something spectacular.

Enter the Golden Hour Salad. This isn't your average green salad; it’s a celebration of peak-season stone fruit, creamy Italian cheese, and the zing of fresh basil from the garden. The secret lies in the contrast between the cold, milky burrata and the warmth of peaches that have spent all day soaking up the sun on the counter. Let’s dive in!

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 0 mins Servings: 2-4

Ingredients

  • 3 large ripe peaches: Freestone varieties work best for easy slicing.
  • 2 balls of fresh Burrata cheese: Approximately 4oz each.
  • 1 cup heirloom cherry tomatoes: Halved, for a punch of acidity.
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves: Large leaves torn, small leaves left whole.
  • 1/4 cup toasted pistachios: Roughly chopped for that essential crunch.
  • 2 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil: Use the good stuff today!
  • 1 tbsp White Balsamic Glaze: Or a honey-infused reduction.
  • Flaky Sea Salt & Fresh Cracked Black Pepper: To taste.
  • Optional: A handful of wild arugula for a peppery base.

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Fruit: Wash the peaches thoroughly. Slice them into 1/2-inch thick wedges. If you prefer a bit of char, you can quickly sear these on a grill pan for 1 minute per side, but raw and juicy is my preference for today.
  2. The Base: If using arugula, scatter it across a wide, shallow platter. If not, start by arranging the peach wedges and halved cherry tomatoes in a loose, overlapping circle.
  3. The Star of the Show: Place the burrata balls in the center of the plate. Use a sharp knife to gently score the top of the cheese in a cross shape, allowing the creamy stracciatella interior to spill out slightly.
  4. The Garnish: Generously scatter the torn basil leaves and toasted pistachios over the entire dish. The green against the orange and white is absolutely stunning.
  5. The Finish: Drizzle the extra virgin olive oil and white balsamic glaze in a slow, circular motion. Finish with a heavy pinch of flaky sea salt and a few cracks of black pepper.
  6. Serve Immediately: This salad is best enjoyed the moment it is assembled while the cheese is cool and the fruit is fragrant.

Pro Tips for Success

The "Finger Test": When picking peaches, give them a very gentle squeeze near the stem. If there’s a slight give, they are ready. If they are rock hard, wait another day!

Temperature Matters: Take the burrata out of the fridge about 20 minutes before serving. It shouldn't be warm, but taking the chill off allows the creamy center to flow better and enhances the flavor.

Substitutions: No pistachios? Toasted pine nuts or slivered almonds work beautifully. If you can't find white balsamic, a drizzle of honey and a squeeze of lemon juice will provide a similar brightness.
