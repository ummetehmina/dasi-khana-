Sun-Drenched Summer: Heirloom Tomato & Grilled Peach Burrata Salad

Happy Saturday, everyone! We are officially in the "Golden Days" of August. The humidity is thick, the cicadas are singing their loudest, and my kitchen counter is currently a mosaic of colors thanks to my morning trip to the farmer’s market. If there is one dish that encapsulates the peak of the season, it is this one.

This salad is all about contrasts: the smoky heat of grilled peaches against the creamy, cool center of burrata cheese, and the acidic snap of heirloom tomatoes balanced by a crunchy, herb-heavy pistachio gremolata. It’s elegant enough for a dinner party but simple enough to assemble when it's too hot to turn on the oven for more than ten minutes. Let's get into the recipe!

Prep Time: 15 mins
Cook Time: 10 mins
Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • For the Salad:
  • 3 large heirloom tomatoes (variety of colors), sliced into thick rounds or wedges
  • 2 firm but ripe peaches, pitted and sliced into thick wedges
  • 2 balls (4 oz each) of fresh Burrata cheese
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, roughly chopped
  • Extra virgin olive oil for drizzling
  • Flaky sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons aged balsamic glaze
  • For the Pistachio Gremolata:
  • 1/4 cup shelled pistachios, toasted and finely chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, grated or finely minced
  • Zest of 1 organic lemon
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped flat-leaf parsley

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Gremolata: In a small bowl, toss together the chopped pistachios, lemon zest, grated garlic, and parsley. Set this aside to let the oils from the lemon zest infuse the nuts.
  2. Grill the Peaches: Lightly brush your peach wedges with a touch of olive oil. Heat a grill pan or outdoor grill to medium-high heat. Place the peaches on the grill for 2-3 minutes per side until distinct char marks appear. Remove and let them cool slightly.
  3. Layer the Base: Arrange the heirloom tomato slices and the grilled peaches on a large, shallow platter. Be sure to overlap them slightly to create depth. Season the tomatoes generously with flaky sea salt and pepper at this stage.
  4. Add the Burrata: Place the burrata balls in the center (or tear them into smaller pieces and nestle them among the fruit). Use a knife to gently break the outer skin of the cheese so the creamy stracciatella center spills out.
  5. The Finishing Touches: Sprinkle the pistachio gremolata over the entire platter. Scatter the torn basil and mint leaves across the top.
  6. Dress: Finish with a generous drizzle of your best quality olive oil and the balsamic glaze. Serve immediately while the peaches are still slightly warm and the cheese is at room temperature.

Pro Tips for the Best Salad

Room Temperature is Key: Never serve your burrata straight from the fridge. Take it out 30 minutes before serving so it reaches its maximum creaminess.

Tomato Selection: Look for "ugly" tomatoes! The ones with deep ridges and varying colors (like Cherokee Purples or Green Zebras) offer the most complex flavor profiles for this dish.

The "Firm" Peach Rule: If your peaches are too soft, they will turn to mush on the grill. Look for fruit that has a slight give but still feels sturdy.
