Miso-Glazed Peach & Halloumi Skewers

Wednesday, August 19, 2026

Happy Wednesday, everyone! We are officially in the "golden hour" of summer. The late August heat is still lingering, but there's a subtle crispness in the evening air that tells us autumn isn't far off. Today, I’m sharing a recipe that perfectly bridges that gap: Miso-Glazed Peach & Halloumi Skewers with Mint Pesto.

This dish is a total flavor explosion. You get the sweetness of sun-ripened peaches, the salty, chewy satisfaction of grilled halloumi, and a savory hit of umami from the white miso glaze. Topped off with a bright, zingy mint pesto, it's the ultimate backyard barbecue centerpiece or a sophisticated weeknight dinner.

Prep Time: 20 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Servings: 4 (8 skewers)

Ingredients

For the Skewers:

  • 3 large, firm-ripe peaches, cut into thick wedges
  • 2 blocks (250g each) Halloumi cheese, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 red onion, cut into chunks
  • 2 tablespoons white miso paste
  • 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil

For the Mint Pesto:

  • 1 cup fresh mint leaves, packed
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro or parsley
  • 1/4 cup toasted pistachios (or pine nuts)
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Glaze: In a small bowl, whisk together the white miso paste, honey, rice vinegar, and sesame oil until smooth. If the paste is too thick, add a teaspoon of warm water to loosen it up.
  2. Assemble Skewers: Thread the peach wedges, halloumi cubes, and red onion chunks onto wooden or metal skewers, alternating as you go. (If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 30 minutes beforehand to prevent burning).
  3. Brush: Generously brush the miso glaze all over the skewers, ensuring the peaches and cheese are well-coated.
  4. Make the Pesto: While the grill heats up, place the mint, cilantro, pistachios, garlic, lemon juice, and red pepper flakes into a food processor. Pulse until finely chopped. With the motor running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil until a vibrant, chunky sauce forms. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Grill: Pre-heat your grill or a grill pan over medium-high heat. Lightly oil the grates. Place skewers on the grill and cook for 3–4 minutes per side. You want the halloumi to be charred and softening, and the peaches to have distinct grill marks and caramelized edges.
  6. Serve: Arrange the skewers on a platter and drizzle immediately with the fresh mint pesto. Garnish with a few extra mint leaves or crushed pistachios.

Pro Tips for Success

  • The Peach Factor: Use peaches that are "firm-ripe." If they are too soft, they will fall off the skewers and turn to mush on the grill.
  • Don't Overcrowd: Leave a tiny bit of space between the ingredients on the skewer so the heat can circulate and caramelize the edges of the cheese.
  • Vegan Option: Swap the halloumi for extra-firm tofu (pressed well) and use maple syrup instead of honey. It’s just as delicious!
