Golden Hour Flatbread

Sunday, August 16, 2026

There is a specific kind of magic in the air today. It’s mid-August, and the sun has that heavy, honeyed glow that signals the peak of summer bounty. My kitchen counter is currently a chaotic mosaic of farmers market finds: ears of sweet corn still cool from the morning dew, a basket of bright green shishito peppers, and a tub of creamy burrata that practically begged to be taken home.

Today’s recipe is a celebration of this "golden hour" of the season. We’re taking the sweetness of summer corn and pairing it with the umami-rich depth of a miso-honey glaze, then piling it all onto a crispy flatbread with blistered shishitos. It’s sweet, salty, slightly spicy, and incredibly indulgent. Grab a glass of chilled white wine; dinner is served.

Miso-Honey Corn & Blistered Shishito Flatbread

Prep Time: 20 mins Cook Time: 15 mins Servings: 2-4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb store-bought or homemade pizza dough, room temperature
  • 2 ears of fresh sweet corn, kernels cut off the cob
  • 1 cup shishito peppers (about 12-15 peppers)
  • 1 ball (8 oz) fresh burrata cheese
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1.5 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Fresh basil leaves and flaky sea salt for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat & Prep: Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If you have a pizza stone, place it in the oven while it heats. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper if not using a stone.
  2. Make the Glaze: In a small bowl, whisk together the melted butter, white miso paste, and honey until smooth. Set aside.
  3. Char the Veggies: Heat the olive oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Toss in the shishito peppers and cook for 3-4 minutes, turning occasionally, until they are blistered and charred in spots. Remove from heat.
  4. Shape the Dough: On a lightly floured surface, stretch or roll your dough into a large oval or rectangle (about 1/4 inch thick). Transfer the dough to your prepared baking sheet or a pizza peel.
  5. Assemble: Brush half of the miso-honey mixture over the base of the dough. Scatter the raw corn kernels and charred shishitos evenly across the top. Sprinkle with red pepper flakes if you want a little extra heat.
  6. Bake: Slide the flatbread into the oven. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the corn is starting to caramelize at the edges.
  7. The Grand Finale: Remove the flatbread from the oven. While still hot, carefully tear the burrata ball into chunks and nestle them among the corn and peppers. The residual heat will make the cheese wonderfully creamy.
  8. Serve: Drizzle the remaining miso-honey glaze over the top. Garnish with torn basil and a generous pinch of flaky sea salt. Slice and enjoy immediately.
Pro Tips:
  • The Shishito Roulette: Remember that about 1 in 10 shishito peppers is surprisingly spicy! It adds an element of fun to the meal.
  • Corn Hack: Don't boil the corn first. Using raw kernels directly on the flatbread allows them to roast and stay "pop-in-your-mouth" crunchy.
  • Dough Temp: Always let your dough sit at room temperature for at least 30 minutes before stretching; it prevents it from snapping back.
