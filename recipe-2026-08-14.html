Happy Friday, everyone! We are officially in the thick of the August heat, and if your kitchen is anything like mine, the counter is currently overflowing with stone fruit. Today, I'm sharing a dessert that perfectly captures the golden hour of summer: Miso-Honey Glazed Peaches with Whipped Mascarpone.

This isn't your average fruit crumble. We're leaning into that "swalty" (sweet and salty) trend by whisking white miso into a honey butter glaze. When it hits the high heat of a cast-iron skillet, the sugars caramelize into a deep, umami-rich crust that cuts right through the floral sweetness of a ripe peach. Topped with a cloud of cold, velvety mascarpone, it’s the ultimate sophisticated-yet-simple summer finale.

Miso-Honey Glazed Peaches

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 large, ripe but firm peaches (yellow or white)
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons mild honey (clover or wildflower)
  • 1 tablespoon white miso paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla bean paste (or extract)
  • 1/2 cup mascarpone cheese, chilled
  • 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream, chilled
  • 1 tablespoon powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons toasted pistachios, crushed (for garnish)
  • Flaky sea salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Glaze: In a small microwave-safe bowl (or a small saucepan over low heat), melt the butter. Whisk in the honey, white miso, and vanilla bean paste until completely smooth. Set aside.
  2. Prep the Peaches: Slice the peaches in half and remove the pits. If the peaches are very large, you can cut them into thick wedges. Brush the cut sides generously with about half of the miso-honey mixture.
  3. Sear the Fruit: Heat a large cast-iron skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat. Once hot, place the peaches cut-side down. Sear for 3-4 minutes without moving them, until they develop deep charred lines and a caramelized crust.
  4. Glaze Again: Reduce the heat to medium. Flip the peaches over. Drizzle the remaining glaze into the pan and over the peaches. Let them simmer in the bubbling glaze for another 2 minutes until the fruit is tender but not mushy.
  5. Whip the Mascarpone: While the peaches cool slightly, combine the mascarpone, heavy cream, and powdered sugar in a medium bowl. Whisk by hand or with an electric mixer until medium peaks form. Do not over-beat, or the mascarpone may grain.
  6. Assemble: Place two peach halves in a shallow bowl. Spoon the warm pan juices over the top. Add a generous dollop of the whipped mascarpone.
  7. Garnish: Finish with a sprinkle of crushed pistachios and a tiny pinch of flaky sea salt to make the flavors pop.
Pro Tips:
  • Peach Selection: Choose peaches that give just slightly when pressed. If they are too soft, they will turn into jam in the pan. If they are too hard, they won't release enough juice.
  • Make it Vegan: Swap the butter for coconut oil, use maple syrup instead of honey, and top with a dollop of chilled coconut cream.
  • Wine Pairing: This pairs beautifully with a chilled glass of Sauternes or a late-harvest Riesling.
