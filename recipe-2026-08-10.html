Late Summer Glow: Grilled Peach and Burrata Panzanella

Monday, August 10, 2026

Happy Monday, everyone! Can you believe we’re already deep into August? The heat outside is relentless, but the produce at the farmer's market has never been better. Today, I’m leaning into that "golden hour" energy with a dish that captures the very essence of summer: Grilled Peach and Burrata Panzanella.

Traditional Italian panzanella is a tomato and bread affair, but when peaches are this juicy and fragrant, they deserve a spot on the grill. We're pairing smoky, caramelized stone fruit with creamy burrata cheese and a spicy hot honey vinaigrette that ties everything together. It’s sweet, salty, crunchy, and creamy—all on one plate.

Prep Time: 20 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • For the Salad:
  • 4 cups sourdough bread, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 3 large ripe peaches, sliced into thick wedges
  • 2 balls of fresh Burrata cheese
  • 1 pint heirloom cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups fresh wild arugula
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  • Olive oil for brushing
  • For the Hot Honey Vinaigrette:
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp white balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust for heat)
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Flaky sea salt and cracked black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Toast the Bread: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Toss the sourdough cubes with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt. Spread on a baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes until golden and crisp. Set aside to cool slightly.
  2. Grill the Peaches: Lightly brush the peach wedges with olive oil. Heat a grill pan or outdoor grill over medium-high heat. Grill the peaches for about 2-3 minutes per side until distinct grill marks appear and the sugars begin to caramelize. Remove and set aside.
  3. Make the Vinaigrette: In a small jar or bowl, whisk together the olive oil, white balsamic, honey, red pepper flakes, and garlic. Season generously with salt and pepper.
  4. Assemble the Base: In a large mixing bowl, combine the toasted bread cubes, cherry tomatoes, and red onions. Drizzle half of the dressing over the mixture and toss well. Let it sit for 5 minutes; this allows the bread to soak up some of the tomato juices and dressing without getting soggy.
  5. Add the Greens: Gently fold in the arugula and grilled peaches.
  6. The Finishing Touch: Transfer the salad to a large serving platter. Tear the burrata balls open and nestle them into the salad. Drizzle the remaining dressing over the cheese and top with fresh basil leaves and an extra sprinkle of flaky sea salt.

Pro Tips for the Perfect Panzanella

  • The Bread Matters: Use a crusty, day-old sourdough if possible. You want the bread to be sturdy enough to hold the dressing without turning into mush.
  • Peach Ripeness: Choose peaches that are fragrant but still slightly firm to the touch. If they are overripe, they will fall apart on the grill.
  • Serve Immediately: This salad is best enjoyed within 30 minutes of assembly to maintain that perfect contrast between the crunchy bread and the juicy fruit.
